### Priority Issues

- **[P2] What**: Overused Typography (Plus Jakarta Sans)
  **Why it matters**: Plus Jakarta Sans is currently one of the most recognizable "AI-generated" fonts. Using it dilutes the brand's unique identity, making the site feel less like a local business and more like a template.
  **Fix**: Switch to a more distinctive or industrial sans-serif that fits the tech repair vibe (e.g., Space Mono for accents, or a sharper sans like Inter Tight or a specific geometric face).
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

- **[P3] What**: Dense Bento Grid Readability
  **Why it matters**: While the glassmorphism looks premium, the sheer amount of text in the service cards can become visually overwhelming on mobile screens.
  **Fix**: Increase negative space within cards and possibly move some secondary bullet points into a tooltip or progressive disclosure.
  **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: The dense bento grid cards, specifically cards like "Manutenção de Notebooks" that span two columns on desktop, might stack into a very long scroll on mobile. Casey may abandon the scroll before reaching "Películas & Capinhas".
